# Voice PE Realtime 0.21.1: no stuck devices, fewer cut-off replies

Add-on version `0.21.1`, 2026-11-09. Full entry in the
[changelog](../../openai_realtime_voice_agent/CHANGELOG.md).

## What changes

- **A stuck session close can't silence a device.** It holds new sessions for
  at most a minute, and the device says it's finishing up instead of reporting a
  usage limit. Repeated open failures back off instead of disabling the device.
- **Reconnects don't wait.** A device that reconnects is served at once.
- **Safer "forget".** It needs a real phrase, asks before removing more than
  two notes, and keeps a backup.
- **Smaller fixes** to the announce endpoint, logging and task handling.

Firmware 1.3.3 adds the device side: it rides out short audio gaps instead of
cutting a reply short.

## Verification

Full test suite (1516 tests), plus real-audio trials on a test device with
firmware 1.3.3: regression 8 of 8 on a clean run, owner confirmation 8 of 8,
stop then next reply, idle close and reconnect. First audio to audible stayed
at 337 ms (336 ms before).
