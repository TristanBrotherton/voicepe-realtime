# Voice PE Realtime 0.21.0: right answers, whole replies

Add-on version `0.21.0`, 2026-11-01. Full entry in the
[changelog](../../openai_realtime_voice_agent/CHANGELOG.md).

## What changes

- **Fewer wrong answers.** Simple state questions are answered locally, a guess
  can't pass as the answer, and announcements or whole-house commands need a
  clear request or a spoken yes.
- **Whole replies.** One speaker queue for everything a device plays on GPT-Live,
  and a wake during a reply or a session close keeps its request.
- **No wrong names from pitch.** With voice prints enrolled, pitch alone doesn't
  name anyone.
- **Stop after a timer** and **less self-waking** need the matching firmware.

## Verification

Full test suite and contract check, plus real-audio trials on a test device,
including a weak-link run. The last regression trial scored 7 of 8; the miss was
the trial reading another device's turn from the shared log.
